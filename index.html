FAQ

Is Lozenge free?

Yes. Lessons, practice and the first mock test of every paper are free. Plus unlocks every mock test, unlimited practice and the live AI examiner.

Is this the official Cambridge app?

No. Lozenge is an independent practice app. Every text, recording and exercise is original, written in the format of the real exam.

Which exams does it cover?

B1 Preliminary, B2 First and C1 Advanced: Reading, Use of English, Listening, Writing and Speaking.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start straight away and your progress stays on your device. A free account syncs it and unlocks AI marking.
