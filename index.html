What's inside.

Over 2,000 exam questions, 740 recordings, 150 listening reels and an AI examiner for Writing and Speaking. Everything is original and written in the exam's format.

Ten minutes a day

Tap the right word, build the sentence, spot the mistake. Tap “Why?” on a wrong answer and the AI explains it in your language – and your mistakes come back just before you'd forget them.

Mock tests and Exam day

Timed papers with the real parts and questions, a score on the Cambridge English Scale for each – or sit a whole test in one go, in the real order with breaks, and get one final grade.

A live AI examiner

Plus: a real spoken conversation with Anna, the examiner – or Part 3 with Tom, another candidate, like on the day. Then your speaking is marked on the official criteria.

Writing and Speaking, marked

Essays, emails and articles marked on the four official criteria, with corrections and a stronger version of your answer. Wrote it by hand? Take a photo. Speaking shows the words to practise saying.

Listening reels

150 short scenes with British voices and video: answer a question, then read the transcript and save new words. Plus full Listening tests at exam speed.

Exam guides

How to write each task and ace every Speaking part, card by card and read aloud – with model answers and the mistakes that cost marks.

Know your grade

Lozenge predicts the grade you'd get today, charts it after every mock test and tells you which paper gains you the most. Pick a goal; every Sunday you get your week in numbers.

For teachers

Create a class, share its code, and see each student's predicted score, weakest paper and when they last practised – never their answers.

In your language

The app speaks 16 languages, from Czech and Slovak to Turkish, Ukrainian and Vietnamese. The exam material stays in English, exactly as you'll meet it on the day.
