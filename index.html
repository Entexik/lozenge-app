    Fair questions

    Why is Plus cheaper on the web?

    The App Store keeps a share of every purchase made inside an app. When you buy here, there is no such share, so the price is lower. You get exactly the same Plus.

    I bought on the web. How do I get Plus in the app?

    Sign in to the app with the same Apple or Google account you used here. Plus turns on by itself, on every device.

    Is this an official Cambridge app?

    No. Lozenge is an independent app for people preparing for Cambridge English exams. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cambridge University Press & Assessment.

    How close is the score to the real one?

    Mock papers are converted with the tables Cambridge publishes for practice tests, so the scale is the same. It is still an estimate from practice papers.

    What happens to my essays and my voice?

    An essay is sent to the AI tutor only after you agree, and your voice goes to the speaking service only while you practise Speaking. The privacy page lists every service by name.

    Is there an Android version?

    Not yet. Lozenge is on iPhone and iPad.
